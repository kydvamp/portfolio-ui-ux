Case study / 02

UtangGo

A simple mobile app that helps sari-sari store owners track customer debts and payments in one place.

View Live Demo

Overview

Making utang easier to manage.

Have you ever had trouble keeping track of customer debts in your store? UtangGo is designed to help sari-sari store owners record debts, track payments, and check unpaid balances without relying on a notebook.

Time Frame

4 days

My Role

UI/UX Design, Visual Design, User Flow, Prototyping

Tools

Figma

01 / The problem

Keeping track of utang can be difficult.

Many sari-sari store owners still use notebooks to record customer debts. As the number of customers grows, it can become harder to find records, remember payments, and keep track of who still owes money. Missing or unclear entries can also lead to confusion between store owners and customers.

02 / The goal

Make debt tracking simple and reliable.

My goal was to create an easy-to-use app that helps store owners manage customer debts without adding more work to their daily routine. I wanted users to quickly record new debts, update payments, and check remaining balances without having to go through pages of handwritten records.

03 / The design solution

A simple way to manage every customer's balance.

UtangGo organizes customer records and debt information in one place. Store owners can select a customer, view their outstanding balance, record new debts, and update payments. The simple layout keeps important information easy to find, even for users who are not familiar with mobile apps.

04 / Key features

01

Customer List

View saved customers and quickly check who has outstanding debts.

02

Debt Recording

Record new purchases made on credit and keep each customer's debt history organized.

03

Payment Tracking

Record customer payments and update their remaining balance.

04

Balance Overview

See total unpaid debts at a glance to keep track of how much customers still owe.

05

Customer Records

Check individual customer details and review their debt and payment history when needed.

05 / Design process

Designing around everyday store needs.

I started by looking at how sari-sari store owners manage utang and the problems they may face when using handwritten records. I defined the main needs of the users and explored ways to make debt tracking faster and easier. I then planned the app's features and user flow, focusing on how store owners could add customers, record debts, and update payments with minimal steps. Finally, I designed the interface with a simple layout and clear labels to make the app easy to understand and use.

06 / Reflection

Turning an everyday problem into a design idea.

Working on UtangGo helped me see how UI/UX design can address problems people face in their daily lives. I learned to focus on the user's actual needs, organize information clearly, and plan simple user flows that make tasks easier to complete. Since the app is intended for sari-sari store owners, I also had to think about keeping the experience straightforward rather than adding unnecessary features. This project helped me practice turning a common problem into a practical app concept while improving my skills in Figma, layout design, and user-centered thinking.
