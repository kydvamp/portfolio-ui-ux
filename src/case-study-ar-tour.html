Client School AR Tour, Advertisement & QR Landing Page

A connected school experience that uses a digital tour, physical touchpoints, and a focused landing page.

View Figma Prototype

Overview

Connecting a first impression to a real visit.

This campaign concept brings together an augmented-reality tour, promotional content, and a QR landing page. The goal is to help prospective students and families discover the school through a short, memorable digital path.

Role

UI/UX Designer
Front-End Developer

Tools

Figma
HTML, CSS

Focus

Campaign consistency
Fast information access

Design direction

One visual language across every touchpoint.

The experience uses a clear route from discovery to action: see the school, learn what makes it distinctive, then take the next step. Strong contrast and concise content keep the landing page useful on any device.
