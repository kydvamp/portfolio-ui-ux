Kaya AI (Study Buddy)

A friendly study companion that turns scattered questions into clear, focused learning moments.

View Figma Prototype

Overview

Making study support feel less intimidating.

Kaya AI is a concept for students who need quick guidance without losing their own voice in the learning process. The experience balances warmth and structure, helping users move from a blank question to a useful next step.

Role

UI/UX Designer
Front-End Developer

Tools

Figma
HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Focus

Conversation flow
Accessible interaction

Design direction

Clear prompts, calm hierarchy, useful feedback.

The interface keeps the next action obvious while giving answers enough breathing room to be read, understood, and acted on. Bright accents add energy without competing with the learning task.
