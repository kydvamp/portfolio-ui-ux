Case Study / 02

UtangGo

A conceptual financial tracking web app designed to help users easily record, manage, and settle personal debts (utang) with friends and family in a seamless interface.

View Figma Prototype

Role

UI/UX Designer
Front-End Developer

Timeline

4 Weeks

Tools

Figma, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

The Problem

Keeping track of borrowed money among friends and family often relies on mental notes or scattered chat messages. This leads to forgotten debts, awkward conversations, and strained relationships. There needed to be a frictionless way to record and track these informal micro-loans.

The Solution

UtangGo centralizes debt tracking by allowing users to quickly log who borrowed what, set gentle automated reminders, and view a simplified dashboard of their total balance (owed vs. owing). The focus was on creating a non-intimidating, friendly interface that removes the stigma of asking for your money back.

Key Features

01

Quick Ledger Entry

A streamlined form to instantly add a new transaction, specifying the person, amount, and a brief note on what the transaction was for.

02

Net Balance Dashboard

A clear, visual representation of your financial standing, immediately showing whether you are currently in the green (people owe you) or red (you owe people).

03

Gentle Nudges

Pre-written, polite reminder templates that can be copied or shared directly to messaging apps to take the awkwardness out of following up.

Reflections & Learnings

Building UtangGo reinforced the importance of emotional design in financial tools. Because money can be a sensitive topic, the UI needed to feel light and accessible. From a technical standpoint, managing the DOM dynamically with JavaScript to update the ledger in real-time was a great exercise in state management.
