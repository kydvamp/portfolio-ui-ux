UtangGo

A straightforward money companion that makes shared debts and repayment plans easier to track.

View Figma Prototype

Overview

Turning awkward money conversations into clear actions.

UtangGo is a finance concept focused on everyday borrowing between friends and family. It gives people a private, low-friction way to record what is owed, understand due dates, and keep agreements visible.

Role

UI/UX Designer
Front-End Developer

Tools

Figma
HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Focus

Trust and clarity
Mobile-first flow

Design direction

Quiet guidance for a sensitive subject.

The visual system uses direct labels, readable numbers, and gentle reminders to make financial information feel manageable. Every screen is built around one clear decision at a time.
