Case study / 01

Kaya AI

A study buddy that brings flashcards, practice quizzes, a focus timer, and an AI tutor into one simple workspace.

View Live Demo

Overview

Your study space, made simpler.

Have you ever found studying challenging? Kaya AI is designed to help you make the most of your study time and create a more effective learning experience.

Time Frame

1 Week

My Role

UI/UX Design, Visual Design, User Flow, Prototyping

Tools

Figma

01 / The problem

Learning looks different for everyone.

Some students struggle to stay focused, get overwhelmed by too many tasks, or find it hard to understand lessons on their own. For students with ADHD or other learning disabilities, keeping up with schoolwork can be even more challenging. Most study tools offer the same experience to everyone, even when students have different needs.

02 / The goal

Make every study session count.

My goal was to bring flashcards, AI quizzes, and a study tutor together in one workspace where everything works hand in hand. I wanted students to move easily from learning a lesson to practicing what they've learned, while staying focused and motivated. With all the tools in one place, studying can feel more organized, rewarding, and easier to keep up with.

03 / The design solution

A clear path from the dashboard to the next task.

Kaya AI is designed around a simple dashboard that gives students a clear view of their study activities. Each tool has its own space, from chatting with the AI tutor to reviewing flashcards and taking quizzes. The sidebar makes it easy to move between features, while the pinned focus timer stays visible during study sessions.

04 / Key features

01

Study Dashboard

View your study progress, access your study tools, and pick up where you left off from one main screen.

02

AI Tutor

Ask questions about difficult topics and get hints, explanations, and step-by-step guidance to help you understand your lessons.

03

Flashcards

Create flashcards from a topic or prompt, flip through them to review key information, and save your decks for later study sessions.

04

Practice Quizzes

Generate multiple-choice quizzes, answer questions, check your score, and read explanations to understand your mistakes.

05

Focus Timer

Use a built-in Pomodoro timer to organize study sessions into focused work and short breaks without leaving the app.

05 / Design process

Designing with students in mind.

I started by understanding the common challenges students face when studying, such as losing focus and having difficulty keeping track of their lessons. I then defined the main problem and explored ideas to make studying easier and more organized. During the ideation stage, I planned the features and how they would work together in one app. Finally, I designed Kaya AI's interface with a simple layout, clear navigation, and a purple and white color palette to create a clean and user-friendly experience.

06 / Reflection

Learning through the design process.

Designing Kaya AI taught me how to turn a simple idea into an app concept with a clear purpose. I learned to organize different study tools into one interface, plan how users move between features, and use colors and layouts to create a consistent design. I also realized that bringing features together is not enough; they need to work well as part of the overall experience. This project helped me improve my Figma and UI/UX design skills while showing me how thoughtful design can make studying easier for students.
