Case study / 03

VR & AR Tour Guide App (Thesis)

A promotional website that helps students, parents, and visitors discover St. John the Baptist Parochial School and easily access its mobile VR campus tour.

View Full Website

Overview

Bringing the campus to your screen.

Checking out a new school can be difficult if you live far away, have a busy schedule, or cannot visit during open houses. The SJBPS VR Tour project introduces a virtual way to explore St. John the Baptist Parochial School from a phone. I designed a promotional website where visitors can learn about the school and quickly access the VR app through a QR code or direct download.

Project Type

Academic / Portfolio Project

My Role

UI/UX Designer

Tools

Figma

01 / The problem

Exploring a school should not require a visit.

Visiting a campus in person can be difficult for busy parents and prospective students who live far away. Photos on social media offer a glimpse of the school, but they cannot fully communicate the layout and feel of its spaces. Accessing the VR app can also be confusing if visitors do not have a clear way to find and download it.

02 / The design goal

Make discovering and accessing the tour simple.

My goal was to design a clean, welcoming landing page that introduces the school and makes the VR tour easy to access. Visitors should be able to understand what the experience offers, scan a QR code, or download the app without navigating through a complicated website.

03 / The design solution

A simple bridge to the virtual tour.

The website focuses on the information visitors need most. An inviting hero section introduces the experience, school value cards highlight what SJBPS stands for, and a dedicated modal provides the QR code and direct app download option. Clear compatibility information helps Android users check whether their device meets the app's requirements before installing it.

04 / Core features

01

Immersive Hero Banner

A welcoming opening section introduces the VR tour and uses a clear call-to-action to guide visitors toward learning more.

02

School Values Cards

Highlight Academic Discipline, Strong School Community, and Character and Values Formation through concise, easy-to-read cards.

03

QR Code Modal

A centered pop-up displays the QR code clearly, allowing desktop visitors to scan it with their phone and access the mobile tour.

04

Direct App Download

A prominent download button gives visitors a direct way to get the app, with Android 8.0 and above compatibility information shown upfront.

05 / Design approach

Designing for a smooth first step.

I focused on making the website easy to understand and navigate across desktop and mobile screens. I organized the content around the school's introduction, core values, and VR app access. The QR code was given a clear place in the modal, while the download button and compatibility notes were designed to make the next step obvious. I also used the school's royal blue and white colors to create a consistent visual identity.

06 / Reflection

Making new technology easier to access.

This project helped me understand that the user experience begins before someone opens an app or starts a virtual tour. People need a clear way to discover the experience and understand how to access it. Designing the landing page taught me to organize information around users' needs, make important actions easy to find, and keep the interface simple without leaving out essential details. It strengthened my UI/UX design skills and showed me how thoughtful web design can support a larger digital experience.
